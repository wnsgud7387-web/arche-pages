Burnpile: Snap & Burn

Contact us

Email archeisit@gmail.com. Please include your device model and app version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Camera access

If you turned camera access off, go to Settings → Burnpile → Camera and turn it on.

Your photos

Photos stay inside the app and are deleted when they burn. To delete everything, burn your pieces or delete the app. We have no server, so we cannot recover deleted photos. See our Privacy Policy.
