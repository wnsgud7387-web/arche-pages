Privacy Policy

Story Accounting: FA, MA & FR · Effective date: 17 October 2026

In short: we do not collect your personal data. The app has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. Your study progress stays on your device. Payments are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

Story Accounting is published by Arche, a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea. Contact: archeisit@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask you to sign up or log in. It does not send your study records, answers, device identifiers, location, contacts or photos to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

3. Information stored only on your device

  • Your study progress, answers and app settings are saved locally on your device so the app can work.
  • We cannot see or access this information.
  • It may be included in your own device backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), which is managed by your account with Apple or Google, not by us.
  • Deleting the app deletes this information from your device.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, card number or billing details. The app only checks with the store whether a purchase is active so it can unlock content. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to their processing.

5. Children

The app is a study aid for professional accounting exams and is not directed at children. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question or request about privacy, including rights you may have under the laws of your country (such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or the Korean Personal Information Protection Act), email us at archeisit@gmail.com and we will reply.

7. Changes

If the app starts to collect any information in the future, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

8. Not affiliated with ACCA

Story Accounting is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ACCA. ACCA is a registered trademark of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants.
